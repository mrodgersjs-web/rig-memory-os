Privacy at the Reckoning

Effective: 2026-08-05

The short version

Gothic Reckoning does not collect, sell, share, or transmit personal information. It has no sign-in, advertising, analytics, tracking, location, contacts, camera, microphone, or photo-library access.

Game data

Offline tutorial-game state remains in the app process and is discarded when you start a new tale or remove the app. It is not sent to us.

Future online play

If you explicitly connect to a hosted OpenViking Werewolf session in a future version, that version will explain what it sends before connection and this policy will be revised before release.

Children

The current offline app does not knowingly collect personal data from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

Material changes will appear on this page before a release that makes them effective. For privacy questions, contact privacy@rodgersintelligence.com.
