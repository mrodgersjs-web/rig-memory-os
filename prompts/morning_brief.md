# Morning Brief Prompt

Used by `founder_runtime.founder_loop.morning_brief` when the model needs to
expand the deterministic brief into prose. The deterministic skeleton already
includes the right sections (queue, focus, sell-ready, killed).

## Section order (handoff §7 — morning brief)

1. What the fleet produced
2. New high-value opportunities
3. Opportunities downgraded or killed
4. Evidence that changed a decision
5. Assets ready for review
6. Blockers needing Mike
7. The three highest-value actions for today
8. Node health and any degraded capacity

The brief must be decision-dense, not activity log. Every line should be
something Mike can act on or ignore.
